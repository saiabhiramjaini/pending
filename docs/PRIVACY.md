# Privacy (stub)

Pending stores payment attempts you start in the app: merchant name, UPI VPA, amount, currency, chosen UPI app, timestamps, and status.

We do **not** collect UPI PIN, OTP, bank passwords, or card numbers. We are not a bank or NPCI payment provider.

You can delete your account in settings (Phase 2). That wipes your rows in our database.

This is a product stub, not legal advice.
